# Risk Policy (Solo Manual Trading)

This is a lightweight, enforceable policy for a small manual account. Keep it simple and follow it 100%.

## Account Context
- Account size: $94 (starting)
- Market: Spot crypto on Coinbase
- Style: Manual execution

## Hard Limits (Do Not Break)
- Max loss per day: **$2** (≈2% of equity)
- Max loss per trade: **$1** (≈1% of equity)
- Max open exposure: **50%** of equity
- Max trades per day: **3**

## Trade Requirements
- Every trade must have:
  - entry, stop, and target
  - defined position size in quote currency
  - reason for entry (one sentence)
- If stop is hit, **stop trading for the day**.

## Do-Not-Trade Conditions
- Illiquid markets (wide spreads, thin order book).
- High‑impact news windows.
- You are tired, rushed, or distracted.

## Compliance
- Record each trade in the journal.
- Daily report must state: rules followed (yes/no) + violations.
- If you break a rule, scale down size next day or skip trading.

## Review Cadence
- Weekly: review violations and update rules if needed (only after 2 weeks of consistent compliance).
